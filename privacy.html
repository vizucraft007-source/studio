Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Channel Studio Uploader ("the app") is a personal desktop tool that runs only on its owner's computer and is used only with the owner's own YouTube channels.

What data the app accesses

With your permission through Google sign-in, the app uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to:

  • upload videos to your channel and set their title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions, playlists and publication time;
  • read the list of your channel's videos and their public statistics;
  • read your channel's YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, retention, traffic sources, audience reports).

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to perform the actions above for you. Access tokens and downloaded statistics are stored only as local files on your own computer. The app has no server: nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. The data is not sold, not used for advertising and not used to train any AI or machine-learning models.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and data

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's folder from your computer deletes all data it has stored.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be asked through the issues section of the GitHub repository that hosts this page.
